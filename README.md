# Welcome to The Midnight Playroom
**Welcome to Midnight Playroom** where haunted dolls find a loving home.
Our dolls may look eerie, but they carry no curse. Each one tells a unique story, waiting for someone brave enough to listen.

## Project Overview
Midnight Playroom is an e-commerce web application that allows users to explore, review, and purchase haunted dolls

## Features
### For user:
- **Browse Haunted Dolls** — View haunted dolls with detailed.
- **Ratings & Reviews** — Share your experience and rate the dolls.
- **Comments & Reviews** — Leave reviews and comments for each doll
- **Shopping Cart & Orders** — Add items to your cart, checkout, and track orders.  
- **User Authentication** — Sign up, log in, and manage your profile securely.
- **Categorized** — Categorized product browsing
### For admin:
- **Product Management** — Add, edit, or remove dolls from the collection.  
- **Admin Dashboard** — View sales data, manage users, and monitor reviews.  
- **Order Management** — Track all customer orders and update their status.
